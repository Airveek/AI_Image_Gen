# Airveek homepage

## Before

The homepage was a long sequence of dark sections with repeated feature explanations and unsupported standalone rating blocks.

## After

The homepage uses one premium signature-gradient hero, a factual trust row, a four-outcome business showcase, a guided workflow with a real walkthrough, one six-category feature slider, decision-oriented pricing and guarantee, FAQ, a full-width final CTA, and footer. It stays compact by letting tabs and sliders carry product breadth.

## Rules

- Keep K2D for display text and Inter for body text.
- Keep white text over the signature gradient and image overlays.
- Use semantic theme tokens everywhere else.
- Use the six generated Soft 3D Studio icons as product-category identifiers.
- Use the four generated outcome visuals to show complete business results, not invented product UI.
- Lead each section with one large, simple decision and explain Airveek in plain business language.
- Do not autoplay the feature slider or walkthrough video.
- Preserve checkout plan keys, route anchors, and the Premium-only wording for perfect-text functionality.
